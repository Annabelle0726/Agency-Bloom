# Agency Bloom

**Your Life. Your Direction. Your Agency.**

Agency Bloom helps people understand their own value, explore possibilities in a changing world, and make choices according to their own wishes.

## Product direction — 2026-10-24

As AI makes many tasks easier and changes the market value of skills, people can still face difficult questions: What matters to me? What kind of life feels worth living? Which possibilities can I create within conditions I cannot fully control?

Bloom supports the person's thinking and decision-making. Practical questions about income, skills and responsibilities belong alongside questions about relationships, interests, values and desired ways of living. A skill's market price is not a complete measure of a person's value; neither personal uniqueness nor a meaningful mission guarantees protection from job displacement.

Four revisitable dimensions express the experience:

| Dimension | The user's question |
| --- | --- |
| **01 · UNDERSTAND** | What do I care about, what have I experienced, and what feels valuable to me? |
| **02 · QUESTION** | Is the premise I currently believe the only possible interpretation? |
| **03 · EXPLORE** | What other possibilities could I investigate or create? |
| **04 · BECOME** | With this new understanding, what do I choose now, including rest or changing my mind? |

These dimensions can be skipped, revisited or combined. They are not a four-step interview that every conversation must complete. Useful advice, ordinary conversation and listening all remain welcome; decisions and saved understandings belong to the user.

Career exploration is the first scenario in which to validate this purpose. Creative work, life choices and preparing a real conversation are related possibilities, not simultaneous commitments to launch new modules. The first evidence of value should be a clearer, user-owned understanding or choice in a concrete situation. Commercial demand still needs separate validation.

**Mission:** We cannot decide what kind of life is worth living for you. We hope to help you see more possibilities, think more clearly, and develop the courage and ability to choose according to your own wishes.

The current product contract, page responsibilities, implementation limits and validation criteria are recorded in [Product direction](docs/PRODUCT_DIRECTION.md). The following essay preserves the project's starting point.

Recently, I've been thinking about one thing: we live in an era of rapid technological development, where AI brings new surprises almost every day. Many tasks that once required long-term study can now be accomplished with tools. Logically, as our capabilities grow stronger, life should become easier and offer more choices. But reality doesn't seem to be exactly that way. Questions like whether work is stable, what to learn in the future, whether current efforts will soon lose value, and what kind of life truly suits us—none of these have disappeared because of technological progress. On the contrary, as the world changes faster and faster, we sometimes can't even understand what happened yesterday before we start worrying about tomorrow. I've experienced this confusion myself. I worked hard to learn new knowledge, tried different technologies and projects, hoping to give myself more choices, only to gradually realize that when a person gets used to constantly pursuing better, even stopping to rest can feel like a waste of time. So I began to ask: are we really striving to get closer to the life we want, or just striving to become an excellent person who meets expectations? This question later became the starting point of my design of Agency Bloom.

## 01·Starting from a conversation, re-understanding your own confusion

Agency Bloom is a WeChat Mini Program I'm developing, currently still in the Alpha stage. It was initially aimed at people who are job hunting, considering a career change, or feeling lost about their future direction.

### 01 · Starting from a conversation

You don't need answers first to start exploring.

I found that when someone says they don't know what to do next, what truly troubles them might not just be a lack of information. Sometimes we've heard plenty of advice, know we should study hard, improve our abilities, and make good plans, yet still don't know which choice is more suitable for us. Because every path has its attractions, and every path has its price to pay, while our wishes, abilities, and reality are often intertwined. I hope Agency Bloom can provide a space where you don't need to rush to an answer. Users can start from their current real confusion, gradually organize their experiences through conversation,

pay attention to things and real constraints, and then understand the possibilities behind different choices.

It doesn't require you to have clear life goals from the start, nor does it require you to package your experiences into a story full of positive meaning. You can admit you don't know what you want, express hesitation about a decision, or just want to carefully sort out recent events. The meaning of exploration is not necessarily to find the right answer immediately, but to give us the opportunity to understand more clearly why we think this way.

## 02·Allowing yourself to grow slowly

Not urging forward, but seeing yourself in this moment.

I once thought that as long as I carefully analyzed the pros and cons of each choice, I could find the most suitable direction. But I gradually realized that some questions cannot be answered entirely by thinking. We might think a job is ideal, only to discover after actually touching it that we don't like that daily life; we might have once overlooked an ordinary lifestyle, only to feel long-lost satisfaction in an accidental experience. So, rather than having AI tell users what to choose, I hope it can help

users discover directions worth exploring, and think about how to gain real experience through affordable small attempts. For example, someone interested in career change doesn't necessarily need to quit immediately or spend months relearning. They can first understand the actual work of a position, try completing a small project, or communicate with practitioners, then re-evaluate their interests and abilities based on these experiences. Agency Bloom hopes to organize such exploration processes through "decision cards," helping users record possible directions, real constraints, risks, and next attempts. AI can provide suggestions, but which content is worth keeping and whether to take action should be confirmed by the user themselves. Because I increasingly feel that good tools shouldn't decide our lives for us, but should help us gain the understanding and space needed to make decisions.

## 03·Life is not a KPI that always needs to be completed

In designing Agency Bloom, I've constantly reminded myself not to make it another efficiency tool that constantly urges users to improve. We already live in an environment that highly values outcomes. Learning needs grades, work needs performance, and personal growth seems to need a clear plan. Even when we start cultivating hobbies, reading, traveling, or resting, we might unconsciously wonder what this period of time has actually brought. Effort is certainly meaningful. Stable income, professional skills, and actual achievements can give us more choices and help us take care of ourselves and important people. I don't think there's anything wrong with pursuing these things. It's just that, if everything in life must prove its value, we might gradually forget that effort was originally meant to make life better, not to make life forever accept the assessment of effort. A person might change goals, or feel exhausted for a period of time. The life once longed for may no longer suit oneself as experiences increase, and this doesn't mean past efforts were all wrong. We should have the opportunity to reconsider, and we should allow ourselves to still enjoy life even without clear outcomes. So I hope Agency Bloom can respect everyone's different pace. It can help users review past choices and record new feelings, but shouldn't understand staying as failure just because the user hasn't acted temporarily. Growth can be a choice, not an obligation that can never be completed.

## 04 · Your life should be in your own hands

The word Agency in the name Agency Bloom points to a concept I cherish very much: the ability of a person, within realistic conditions, to still understand their situation, express their will, and try to make choices. We cannot control everything. Family background, economic conditions, social environment, and how important people view us can all influence the direction of life. But I hope that, even facing these conditions that cannot be completely changed, we can still leave a little space to seriously ask ourselves: what do I really want now? Why do I want it? Does this life really suit me? I also hope the product itself can reflect this respect. AI's understanding of users should not be a permanent and unchanging label. Past wishes may have changed, and once-recognized advice may no longer apply. Users should be able to view and correct their own information, and decide which content is worth saving and which suggestions are just for reference. This is why I hope Agency Bloom's conversations, records, and decision-making processes can leave as much choice as possible in the hands of users. It is not a psychotherapy tool, nor does it hope to become a substitute for real interpersonal relationships. I hope it is a tool to assist exploration, allowing us to hear our own thoughts again between information expectations and various suggestions.

## 03·Leaving the right to choose to users

Suggestions are for reference only; decisions are always confirmed by you.

## 05·A seed that is sprouting

The current Agency Bloom is still young, many designs are still being adjusted, and the real user experience and actual value need further verification. I don't know what it will eventually develop into, nor do I know whether it can answer those complex questions about life direction. But I'm willing to start from a small attempt. I hope to invite some people facing career choices and life changes to participate in the experience in the future, understand what difficulties they truly encounter, which conversations can help them form new understandings, and which suggestions are indeed useful in real life. If after using it, users just get a seemingly perfect plan but still don't know why they live this way, then I think this is not the product I truly hope to create. What I look forward to more is: maybe someone, after a conversation, is finally willing to seriously understand a direction they once wanted to try; maybe someone discovers that a goal they've been pursuing all along is actually not what they want most right now; or maybe someone can finally admit that they've been really tired lately, and temporary rest doesn't mean giving up the future. These changes may not be grand, nor necessarily measurable with beautiful numbers, but they could all become the beginning of re-understanding oneself. I always love a sentence: "I still want to live according to my own will for a while." In an era of constant acceleration, I hope technology can not only help us complete tasks faster, but also give us more opportunities to understand ourselves and discover things in life truly worth cherishing. We don't have to wait until we're excellent enough, successful enough, or finally have a certain future, before allowing ourselves to live well. Perhaps, ideal life does not only exist at some distant endpoint—it also exists in those small and real choices we make today. Agency Bloom, may everyone grow slowly at their own pace.

"☑One small step can also lead to a brighter tomorrow."
