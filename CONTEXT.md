# Agency Bloom

Agency Bloom helps people understand their value, explore possibilities, and make choices according to their own wishes in a changing world. Career exploration is its first validation scenario.

## Language

**Agency**: A person's capacity to understand their circumstances, express their wishes, consider possibilities and make or revise choices within real constraints.
_Avoid_: Productivity, career fit, AI autonomy

**Thinking dimension**: One of Understand, Question, Explore and Become: revisitable ways of seeing oneself, examining a premise, discovering possibilities and choosing how to live. A dimension is not a compulsory stage, user category or permanent trait.

**Exploration**: A continuing question with user-confirmed knowledge, choices and experiments. It can contain several conversations.
_Avoid_: Chat, window

**Conversation**: A bounded discussion with its own messages, response preference and reviewed summary. It belongs to one primary exploration and can explicitly reference other explorations.
_Avoid_: Exploration, decision card

**Conversation summary**: A user-reviewed account of a discussion, including unresolved questions. It helps that conversation resume and is not automatically a long-term fact.

**Memory**: A user-confirmed, editable piece of durable context, scoped to all explorations or one exploration. Its use can be disabled independently of retaining it.

**Decision card**: The user's current confirmed record for an exploration, separating facts, constraints, hypotheses and unknowns.

**Archive**: Removing a conversation from the active conversation list while preserving permitted records. It does not end the exploration or delete the conversation.
